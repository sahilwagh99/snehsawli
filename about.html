An introduction

Who we are

The Mandal is a charitable organisation registered with the Charity Commissioner (Reg. No. E 7129 / Pune) that has served society through social and humanitarian work for the past 40 years.

Over these years it has organised more than 1,800 voluntary blood donation camps, through which more than 2 lakh people have donated blood. Its best-known initiative is Snehsavali – Aapla Ghar, a completely free old-age home in the Pimpri-Chinchwad area.

Our old-age home

Snehsavali – Aapla Ghar स्नेहसावली – आपलं घर

At Charholi, near Alandi, Snehsavali gives 25 destitute and unsupported elders a home with shelter, care, dignity and a loving environment, completely free of cost.

Accommodation

A safe, clean home with round-the-clock care.

Nutritious food

Wholesome meals served every day.

Clothing

Clothes and daily essentials for every resident.

Medical care

Comprehensive healthcare, including surgeries.

Treatments the Mandal pays for

Beyond daily care, the Mandal bears the cost of essential medical procedures for its residents, such as:

  • Bypass surgery
  • Cataract surgery
  • Hip replacement
  • Other necessary treatments
Supporting the cause

Raddi Daan & Bhangar Daan

Waste paper and scrap collected from households is turned into funds for the old-age home and our other charitable work.

It is one of several fundraising efforts that keep Snehsavali free for its residents. Volunteers, including Dr. and Mrs. Vaidya themselves, go door to door to collect old newspapers and scrap, and this income alone covers more than half of the home’s annual running costs.

  • Old newspapers, magazines, books and notebooks
  • Cardboard, plastic, iron and other household scrap
  • Picked up from your door, at no cost to you

Our idol, your price

Murti Amchi Kimmat Tumchi मूर्ती आमची – किंमत तुमची

Each Ganesh festival, finely crafted idols made from natural Shadu Mati clay and painted only with natural colours are put on display. Devotees choose the idol they like and contribute any amount they wish, even anonymously.

The funds raised go to the running of the old-age home and our other social and humanitarian work. The initiative aims to:

  • Promote eco-friendly Ganesh idols and a greener festival
  • Give devotees high-quality, natural idols
  • Bring the community together for social causes
  • Raise support for the Mandal’s charitable work
