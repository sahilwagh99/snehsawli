Who we are

Four decades of seva for Pimpri-Chinchwad

Shree Shankar Maharaj Seva Mandal is a registered charitable organisation founded by Dr. Avinash Vaidya and Dr. Mrs. Manali Vaidya out of deep devotion to Shri Shankar Maharaj.

For 40 years the Mandal has organised voluntary blood donation camps, cared for destitute senior citizens, and supported poor patients and students. Its work rests on four core principles:

  • Environmental Protection
  • Service to Humanity
  • Spirituality
  • Nationalism

Snehsavali – Aapla Ghar

स्नेहसावली – आपलं घर

A completely free old-age home at Charholi, near Alandi. Residents receive accommodation, nutritious food, clothing and full medical care, including surgeries such as bypass, cataract and hip replacement.

Our next chapter

Building our own old-age home at Mamurdi

4,000sq. ft. of land purchased

The Mandal has bought land at Mamurdi, within the Pimpri-Chinchwad Municipal Corporation area, for a permanent home dedicated to senior citizens.

Construction is scheduled to begin shortly. It is the biggest step yet towards giving elders in need lasting care, dignity and shelter.
